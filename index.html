Methodology. Cost savings compare contractor cost against the fully loaded FTE-equivalent cost of the team that would otherwise perform the work, using standard comparison rates by team (rate detail available from Solutions Ops leadership). "Output tickets" (3,822) exclude 580 meeting/training-titled entries so the count reflects delivered work; those entries’ hours remain in contractor hours and cost savings. Figures cover completed contractor tickets Feb 2 – Jul 31, 2026 from the Master 27 dataset; admin/overhead hours (3,435) are tracked separately and excluded from ticket counts, client metrics, and savings. Clients-served counts exclude 12 internal labels found in the customer field (Internal Ops, SC, SC Team, HR Team, Professional Services, RFx Support, Agentic AI, etc.) and merge near-duplicate names — 878 unique external clients; the work-area breakdown excludes Internal Ops Support (Process Improvement) and merges duplicate labels to canonical forms. Active contractors = distinct contractors completing tickets in July 2026 (54 distinct contractors contributed FYTD); prior figure per FY26 year-end reporting. Environment counts (564 FYTD) include completed tickets plus those in final review as of Aug 13, 2026 (Clone Environments now merged in), split across client (244), internal enablement (55), and HR interview (265) environments. Prior-year (H1 2025) savings baseline of $215,164 uses last year’s rate convention; FY26 full-year total was $512,441.
